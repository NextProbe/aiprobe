# Code of Conduct

*[中文版](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.zh-CN.md)*

## Our pledge

We want AIProbe to be a project where anyone can report a bug, ask a question,
or propose a change without having to be brave about it. Contributors of every
level of experience and background are welcome.

## Expected behaviour

- Be direct about code and kind about people. Critique the change, not the author.
- Assume the other person is competent and acting in good faith. Most disagreements
  in this repository are about tradeoffs, not about who is right.
- Explain reasoning. "This is wrong" is not review; "this breaks the unknown/zero
  distinction, here is why that matters" is.
- Accept that a well-implemented change can still be declined for scope reasons.
  The project's scope is deliberately narrow, so this is never personal.
- Respect that people contribute on their own time. Nobody owes you a reply on a
  schedule.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, public or private.
- Personal or political attacks, or comments about someone's identity,
  characteristics or background.
- Publishing others' private information without permission. Note that session
  transcripts contain prompts and source code: never paste someone else's, and be
  careful with your own.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or deliberate bad-faith argument.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, commit
messages — and when representing the project in public.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour by opening a
[private report](https://github.com/NextProbe/aiprobe/security/advisories/new)
or contacting a maintainer directly. Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers will respond with what they judge proportionate, from a private word to
a permanent ban. Maintainers who do not uphold this document in good faith should
expect the same standard applied to them.
